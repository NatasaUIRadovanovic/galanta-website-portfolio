Redesign

PawStay was restructured around how people book pet care — fewer competing priorities, sharper hierarchy, one path from discovery to sent request.

What users needed

  • Trust first — verified sitters, reviews, pricing upfront.
  • One job per screen — no dashboard dump on day one.
  • Honest states — request sent ≠ booked.

Design outcomes

Clearer home

Intent-first categories, one featured sitter.

Trust on profile

Reviews & verification before book.

Booking that adds up

Summary total + pay after accept.
