About

Galanta helps startups and growing businesses turn complex ideas into scalable digital products — through product design, design systems, and UX strategy.

Product designer with 8+ years of experience, from early-stage startups to enterprise platforms. Redesigning live products is a core strength — taking apps and sites that have outgrown their UX and making them clearer, faster to use, and ready to ship. Also strong in simplifying complex workflows, design systems, and intuitive experiences for web and mobile.

Works with cross-functional teams, mentors designers, and turns business requirements into products that are usable and technically feasible. Focus areas include white-label platforms, SaaS, mobile apps, and web applications — much of it under NDA, which is why public case studies like PawStay, Spona Sales, and UXZGB are here.

AI is part of the daily workflow — advanced prompt engineering, research acceleration, UX writing, rapid prototyping, and documentation — to move faster without skipping the thinking that makes a product worth using.
